# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment
We aim to keep participation in this project respectful, constructive, and welcoming.

## Expected behavior
- Be respectful and considerate.
- Give actionable feedback focused on the work.
- Accept differing viewpoints and discuss technical disagreements professionally.
- Respect privacy and do not publish confidential customer or business information.

## Unacceptable behavior
Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, and disruptive conduct are not acceptable.

## Enforcement
Project maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions that violate this code and may restrict participation when necessary. Report serious incidents privately through the repository's security contact process.
